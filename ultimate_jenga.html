❓ HOW TO PLAY

🌋 The Setup

A giant Jenga tower stands on a stone platform in a sea of lava. Every survivor starts on the very top. The only safe ground is the platform under the tower, and there's a single 🔴 WIN button right in its center.

🧍 Survivors

Climb, drop and squeeze your way down through the gaps the controller knocks open. Press the WIN button to win! Getting hit by a thrown object, or touching lava, eliminates you.

🎯 Controller

Drive the launch ramp around the tower, set its height, angle and speed, then hurl cars, coins, pencils, bottles, rubber ducks and more. Knock everyone into the lava to win!

📏 Tower Size

At size N, each layer has N blocks, each block is N times longer than it is wide, and the tower is 6×N layers tall. Size 3 is the classic 18-layer Jenga.

💬 Chat & Leaving

Press Enter to chat. Use the 🚪 Leave button any time to leave the match cleanly.
